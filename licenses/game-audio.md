# Game sound credits

The game includes an edited bank of 17 sound effects made from the following Freesound recordings. Each creator dedicated their source recording to the public domain under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Source pages and license designations were checked on 22 November 2026.

| Game sound | Source recording | Creator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rifle | [Sniper Shot](https://freesound.org/people/LeMudCrab/sounds/163460/) | LeMudCrab |
| Twin SMGs | [Authentic M60 Firing.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/FranklinRook1984/sounds/538302/) | FranklinRook1984 |
| Vulcan | [minigun_ver1.wav](https://freesound.org/people/rob762x51/sounds/85246/) | rob762x51 |

| Equipment latch and crate detail | [small metal clasp (open, close, switching)](https://freesound.org/people/_sinny_/sounds/822397/) | _sinny_ |

The source recordings were trimmed, filtered, retimed, layered and mixed for this game. The pitched cues use notes arranged from a recorded glockenspiel strike. The release embeds the resulting mono MP3 bank for offline playback.
