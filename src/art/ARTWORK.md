# Campaign artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool generated one original three-panel illustration. Each strip was cropped for its operation card and boss announcement.

## Generation prompt

Original stylized 3D cinematic key art triptych for a zombie island apocalypse arcade game. One landscape 3:2 image divided horizontally into THREE equal-height full-width panoramic strips stacked vertically with clean invisible boundaries. Each strip is a separate widescreen campaign thumbnail showing a different giant mutated boss on the RIGHT, looking down over an advancing horde of olive green undead and tiny cobalt-blue helmeted survivors with a blue-and-gold tank. Top strip: turquoise coastal bay, weathered red-and-white lighthouse, giant mossy green broad-shouldered zombie in rusted industrial armor with amber glowing eyes on right. Middle strip: smoldering volcanic port at sunset, enormous ochre infected furnace brute with glowing molten shoulder crystals on right. Bottom strip: arctic research harbor, huge pale grey-blue frost zombie with cyan ice growths on right. Very strong graphic silhouettes readable as thumbnails, cinematic lighting and mist shafts, hand-painted 3D hero shooter key art, slightly playful chunky proportions, gorgeous high-end videogame promotional illustration. No gore, no logos, no letters, no text, no type, no border, not a UI. Each strip has one distinct focal silhouette of a different monster on its right side.

## Playable battlefield

The original in-game art was generated for this project. Three overhead road paintings show a flooded coastal causeway, a scorched refinery and a snowbound evacuation route. Perspective projection scrolls each painting under the formation. A transparent character sheet supplies four rear-facing equipment tiers, two infected runners, an armored brute and the boss. A separate equipment sheet supplies the rifle, paired SMGs, rotary cannon and rocket launcher shown on crates, in pickup announcements and in the squad loadout.

The runtime uses individually cropped WebP assets. [Game artwork](game/)

## Character animation

The built-in ImageGen tool created the running, attack and fall sheets. Each pose has a common foot anchor for playback on the road. The three boss sheets contain a four-pose walk, a brace, an overhead wind-up, a ground strike and a recovery. Dedicated fall sheets show each soldier loadout and enemy type staggering, falling sideways and settling on the ground.
